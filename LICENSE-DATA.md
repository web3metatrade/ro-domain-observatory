# Data licensing

The database structure and original observations produced by this project are
intended for open publication under ODbL 1.0. This statement does not override
the rights or terms of upstream sources.

The ODbL grant applies to the project's original schema, consolidation and DNS
measurements to the extent the maintainer can license them. Domain names and
other facts may not themselves be copyrightable, but that does not erase any
contractual terms or database rights that may apply to an upstream compilation.
No upstream source is relicensed by this file.

Every exported row must retain enough provenance to identify its contributing
sources. The curated export under `data/public/` excludes sources marked
`unclear`, `restricted`, `research_only` or `metadata_only` by default. A
separately identified complete measurement release may contain independently
collected DNS results for candidates learned from those sources. Such a release
must retain candidate-source provenance and must not represent the candidate
list as having been discovered by brute force or as being covered by this ODbL
grant.

Important upstream conditions include:

- Common Crawl: Common Crawl Terms of Use and attribution.
- Majestic Million: CC BY 3.0 attribution to Majestic.
- OpenStreetMap: ODbL 1.0 and contributor attribution.
- Wikidata: CC0 1.0.
- Overture Maps: its data license and source-specific attribution.
- Scrape The World: direct permission to merge the supplied dataset into the
  maintainer's public dataset; the confirmation is held by the maintainer.

No WHOIS personal data is included in phase-one exports.
