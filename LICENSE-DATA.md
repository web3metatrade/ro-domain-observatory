# Data licensing

The database structure and original observations produced by this project are
intended for open publication under ODbL 1.0. This statement does not override
the rights or terms of upstream sources.

Every exported row must retain enough provenance to identify its contributing
sources. Sources marked `unclear`, `restricted`, `research_only` or
`metadata_only` in `sources.json` are excluded from public exports by default.
Their observations can be used internally for coverage comparison only until
the maintainer confirms appropriate redistribution permission.

Important upstream conditions include:

- Common Crawl: Common Crawl Terms of Use and attribution.
- Majestic Million: CC BY 3.0 attribution to Majestic.
- OpenStreetMap: ODbL 1.0 and contributor attribution.
- Wikidata: CC0 1.0.
- Overture Maps: its data license and source-specific attribution.
- Scrape The World: direct permission to merge the supplied dataset into the
  maintainer's public dataset; the confirmation is held by the maintainer.

No WHOIS personal data is included in phase-one exports.
